Exercise 3 - OpenMP Master Directive

The #pragma omp master directive specifies that the structured block
must be executed only by the master thread, which is always thread 0.

Therefore, "Inside Master" and "Exiting Master" are printed exactly
once by thread 0. The statements outside the master block are executed
by every thread in the parallel region.

Unlike the single directive, the master directive does not contain an
implicit barrier at its end. Therefore, the other threads do not have
to wait for thread 0 to complete the master block. They may continue
and print their "Hi again" messages while thread 0 is executing the
master block.

The order of the output may change between executions because the
threads execute concurrently.
