Exercise 1 - OpenMP Barrier

The #pragma omp barrier directive creates a synchronization point
inside the parallel region. Each thread first prints that it has
reached the barrier. A thread that reaches the barrier must wait
until every other thread in the parallel region has also reached
the same barrier.

After all threads reach the barrier, they are released and continue
executing the statements that follow it. Therefore, all "reached the
barrier" messages appear before any "passed the barrier" messages.

The exact order of the thread numbers may change between executions
because OpenMP threads run concurrently and their scheduling order is
not guaranteed.
